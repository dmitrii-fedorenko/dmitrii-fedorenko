# Dmitrii Fedorenko
**Founder, CEO & Technical Lead · Opyt Bez Opyta**

I build software for a practical problem: helping students get their first paid work experience while helping businesses complete clearly defined tasks.

I develop the platform software and manage its technical infrastructure, alongside leading the product and business.

## What I am building
**[Opyt Bez Opyta](https://github.com/dmitrii-fedorenko/opyt-bez-opyta)** is an AI-enabled HRTech marketplace connecting students and businesses — the first stage of a broader student ecosystem for international markets. Its code includes task workflows, student and company accounts, submissions and revisions, ratings, portfolios, calendars and subscription-based access rules.

The current RC3.7 build is at the testing / pre-launch stage. Financial-provider workflows and external-service validation remain in progress. Planned features are distinguished from implemented code in the project overview.

## Founding team and international direction
I co-founded Opyt Bez Opyta with **Sofia Shkop — Co-founder & COO**, who leads operations and international development. I lead software development, technical infrastructure, product and business strategy.

AI is a core product direction, with integration code for submission review, support assistance and candidate analysis. External AI processing and quality validation remain part of release preparation.

We passed the first selection stage of a startup program in Dubai and agreed a partnership with **Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU)**. University collaboration is planned after establishing the platform in Primorsky Krai.
